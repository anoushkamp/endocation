## First paper: 11 September

Endocation's first research breakdown will be published on **11 September 2026**.

Every week, I'll take a recently published paper in endometriosis or women's health and break down what the researchers actually found, what the evidence means, where the limitations are, and why it matters.

No sensational headlines. No pretending one study changes everything. Just the science, translated.

## What to expect

Each breakdown will look at the study itself rather than just the abstract or press release.

We'll talk about what the researchers did, what they found, what they **didn't** prove, and how the findings fit into the wider evidence.
